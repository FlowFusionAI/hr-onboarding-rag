# BrightPath Technologies — New Hire FAQs

**Owner:** People & Culture | people@brightpath.io
**Last updated:** January 2026

These FAQs answer the questions most commonly asked by new employees in their first 90 days. If your question isn't here, ask your buddy, your line manager, or email people@brightpath.io.

---

## Pay and Payroll

**Q: When do I get paid?**
Salary is paid on the **last working day of each month** directly to your bank account. Your first payslip will arrive at the end of your first full month of employment. If you start part-way through a month, your first payment covers the prorated days worked since your start date plus the full following month.

**Q: I haven't received my payslip — what do I do?**
Payslips are emailed to your company email address and also available on hr.brightpath.io under "My Payslips". If it is not there by the last working day of the month, email finance@brightpath.io immediately — do not wait until the next day.

**Q: I need to change my bank details — how?**
Log into hr.brightpath.io, go to "My Profile → Payment Details" and update your bank account details. Changes submitted before the **15th of the month** take effect for that month's payroll. Changes after the 15th take effect the following month.

**Q: What happens with my tax code?**
If you have a P45 from a previous employer, upload it to hr.brightpath.io within your first week. If you don't have a P45, complete the Starter Checklist (also on hr.brightpath.io). If HR does not receive one of these, HMRC defaults to an emergency tax code (0T), which means you will pay more tax in your first month. It can be reclaimed, but it is easier to avoid.

## Leave

**Q: How do I book annual leave?**
Submit all leave requests via the HR portal at hr.brightpath.io. Give at least 10 working days' notice for one week or less; four weeks' notice for longer periods. Do not book travel until you receive written approval from your manager.

**Q: How many days of annual leave do I have?**
25 days per year plus UK public holidays. In your first year, leave accrues monthly — if you join mid-year, you will have a prorated entitlement. You can see your current balance at any time on hr.brightpath.io.

**Q: Can I carry over unused leave?**
Yes, up to 5 days can be carried into the next leave year. Carried-over days expire on 31 March of the year they are carried into. Any unused leave beyond 5 days is forfeited at 31 December.

**Q: What do I do if I am sick?**
Call or message your line manager directly before your usual start time on day 1. Do not just send an email. For absences of 1–7 days, submit a self-certification form on hr.brightpath.io within 24 hours of your return. For absences of 8 days or more, you need a fit note from your GP or 111 and must email it to people@brightpath.io on the day you receive it.

**Q: When does enhanced sick pay apply?**
Enhanced sick pay (up to 10 days per year at full pay) only applies once your probation period is passed. During probation, Statutory Sick Pay (SSP) applies from day 1 of illness at the current SSP rate (£116.75/week as of April 2025).

## Equipment and IT

**Q: What equipment will I receive?**
All employees receive a standard bundle: laptop (MacBook Air M2 or Dell XPS 13, depending on preference declared at offer stage), a 24" monitor, keyboard, mouse, and USB-C hub. If you work remotely, the equipment is shipped to your home address.

**Q: I need an extra monitor / headset / standing desk — can I get one?**
Additional peripherals can be requested via the IT portal at it.brightpath.io. Requests under £100 are approved by your manager. Requests over £100 require Finance approval as well. Your learning budget (£1,000/year) cannot be used for equipment — this is separate.

**Q: My laptop has a problem — who do I contact?**
Raise a ticket at it.brightpath.io. For urgent issues (laptop won't start, can't access critical systems), Slack `#it-helpdesk` and someone from IT will respond within 2 hours during core hours (10:00–16:00 UK time).

**Q: I lost my access card — what do I do?**
Report the loss immediately to your office's reception and to security@brightpath.io. A replacement card will be issued within 1 working day. There is no charge for a first replacement; subsequent replacements cost £10.

**Q: Can I install software on my work laptop?**
You can install anything on the approved software list (available at it.brightpath.io/approved-software) without prior approval. Any software not on the list requires a request to it@brightpath.io, which is reviewed within 3 working days. Do not install unapproved software — this is a policy violation.

## Working Arrangements

**Q: How many days do I need to be in the office?**
A minimum of two days per week. There is no fixed requirement for which days — coordinate with your team. During your first 4 weeks, we recommend being in the office more frequently to help you build relationships.

**Q: Can I work fully remotely?**
Fully remote arrangements (zero office days) require a formal request to people@brightpath.io. A minimum six-month tenure is required before you can apply. These requests are reviewed quarterly and are not guaranteed.

**Q: Can I work from abroad?**
Short-term overseas working (up to 14 days per calendar year) is permitted without approval, provided it does not create a tax or employment law issue in the destination country. For anything beyond 14 days, you must get written approval from your line manager and People & Culture before travelling. Working from outside the UK without approval is a disciplinary matter.

**Q: I want to work compressed hours — is that possible?**
Compressed working weeks are available, but only after your probation period is passed. You will need written approval from your line manager and HR. Compressed arrangements are reviewed annually.

## Benefits and Expenses

**Q: When does my private health insurance start?**
Private health insurance with AXA PPP begins on the date your probation is passed. You will receive an enrolment link via email when HR confirms your probation result. Cover includes two dependants at no extra cost.

**Q: How do I claim expenses?**
Register on Spendesk at expenses.brightpath.io using your company email. Submit claims within 30 days of the expense. Claims older than 30 days will not be reimbursed without a written exception approved by the Finance Director. All claims require itemised receipts — card statements alone are not accepted.

**Q: I want to use my learning budget — what's the process?**
Discuss the course, conference, or certification with your line manager first and get their approval in writing (a Slack message or email is fine). Then book it and claim it via expenses.brightpath.io, attaching the approval and the receipt. The £1,000 resets each calendar year and does not roll over.

**Q: Is there a cycle to work scheme?**
Yes. BrightPath offers the scheme via Cyclescheme, available year-round. The maximum tax-free bike value is £1,000. Email finance@brightpath.io to start an application.

## Probation

**Q: What is the probation period?**
3 months from your start date. There is an informal check-in at Month 2 and a formal review at Month 3 attended by your manager and someone from People & Culture.

**Q: What happens if my probation is extended?**
If there are concerns at the Month-3 review, probation can be extended once, by up to 6 additional weeks. You will receive a written explanation of the concerns and clear objectives for the extension period. A further formal review is held at the end of the extension.

**Q: Can I be dismissed during probation?**
Yes. Notice during probation is 1 month from either side, as stated in your contract. If probation is not passed, you will receive written confirmation and your final working day will be communicated at that time.

## Miscellaneous

**Q: I need a reference — who provides it?**
HR provides employment confirmation references only (confirming dates, job title, and that you are/were employed). Personal references from line managers are provided in a personal capacity and require your consent. Contact people@brightpath.io to request an employment reference letter.

**Q: I have a conflict of interest to declare — what do I do?**
Send an email to people@brightpath.io describing the potential conflict before you engage in the relevant work. Conflicts include: financial interest in a client or supplier, a close personal relationship with a client contact, secondary employment or consultancy in the same space. Failure to declare is a disciplinary matter.

**Q: Is there a dress code?**
Smart casual in the office. There is no formal dress code, but use common sense when meeting clients or presenting at events. If you are unsure, ask your manager.

**Q: I'm struggling — where can I get confidential support?**
The Employee Assistance Programme (EAP) via Health Assured offers free, confidential counselling and support (up to 6 sessions per year). Call 0800 028 0199, available 24/7. Your manager and HR are not informed if you use this service.
