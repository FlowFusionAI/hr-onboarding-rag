# BrightPath Technologies — Employee Handbook

**Version:** 3.2 | **Effective date:** 1 January 2026 | **Owner:** People & Culture

---

## 1. About BrightPath

BrightPath Technologies is a UK-based software consultancy founded in 2015, specialising in AI integration, data engineering, and cloud migration. We have approximately 180 employees across three offices: London (HQ, Clerkenwell), Manchester, and Edinburgh. Our mission is to make enterprise software work for people, not the other way around.

This handbook applies to all full-time employees on permanent contracts. Contractors and agency workers should refer to their individual agreements.

Questions about this handbook: people@brightpath.io

## 2. Working Hours and Flexibility

**Core hours:** 10:00–16:00 UK time, Monday to Friday. You are expected to be reachable during these hours on Slack unless you have notified your manager in advance.

**Full working week:** 37.5 hours per week. How you arrange the remaining hours around core hours is up to you and your manager.

**Flexible start:** You may start as early as 07:30 or as late as 10:00, provided your daily hours total 7.5.

**Compressed weeks:** Available after your probation period ends (see Section 7). You must get written approval from your line manager and HR before starting a compressed arrangement.

**Overtime:** BrightPath does not pay overtime. Time off in lieu (TOIL) is available at manager discretion for exceptional project demands. TOIL must be taken within the same calendar quarter it is earned.

## 3. Remote and Hybrid Work

**Default arrangement:** Employees are expected to be in their nearest office at least **two days per week**. There is no fixed day requirement; coordinate with your team.

**London office address:** 14 Farringdon Road, London EC1A 1BB. Entry is via the main reception; collect your permanent access card from the IT desk (Ground Floor, Room G4) within your first three days.

**Manchester office address:** Suite 7, Piccadilly Tower, 1 Piccadilly Gardens, Manchester M1 1RG.

**Edinburgh office address:** 2nd Floor, 110 St Mary's Street, Edinburgh EH1 1SX.

**Full remote requests:** Employees who wish to work fully remotely (zero office days) must submit a formal request to people@brightpath.io. These are reviewed quarterly and are not guaranteed. A minimum six-month tenure is required before applying.

**Equipment for remote working:** BrightPath provides a standard equipment bundle to all employees (laptop, monitor, keyboard, mouse). Additional peripherals require manager approval and must be requested via the IT portal at it.brightpath.io. The standard equipment remains BrightPath property; you must return all items within five working days of leaving.

**Internet allowance:** Employees working remotely receive a £30/month internet contribution, paid as part of salary, not as expenses. This begins in your second month of employment.

## 4. Annual Leave

**Entitlement:** 25 days per year, plus UK public holidays (8 days in England; 9 in Scotland). Leave year runs 1 January to 31 December.

**Accrual in first year:** Leave accrues monthly. If you start on 1 April, you accrue 25 × (9/12) = 18.75 days for the remainder of that leave year, rounded up to 19 days.

**Booking leave:** Use the HR portal (hr.brightpath.io) to submit leave requests. Give at least **10 working days' notice** for one week or less; **four weeks' notice** for anything longer.

**Approval:** Leave requests are approved by your line manager. Requests are typically answered within three working days. You should not book travel until you receive written approval.

**Carry-over:** A maximum of **5 days** may be carried into the following year. Carried-over days expire on 31 March. Unused leave beyond 5 days is forfeited.

**Bank holidays:** If you are required to work on a UK public holiday, you receive a substitute day to be taken within 90 days.

**Leave during notice period:** Leave may not normally be taken during a notice period without manager approval.

---

## 5. Sick Leave

**Entitlement:** Up to **10 days per rolling 12 months** at full pay. After 10 days, Statutory Sick Pay (SSP) applies in accordance with UK law (currently £116.75/week as of April 2025).

**Notification:** Call or message your line manager before your normal start time on the first day of absence. Do not just send an email — a direct message or call is required.

**Self-certification:** Absences of 1–7 calendar days are self-certified. Submit a self-certification form via hr.brightpath.io within 24 hours of returning to work.

**Fit note:** For absences of 8 calendar days or more, you must provide a fit note from your GP or 111 service. Email a scan to people@brightpath.io on the day you receive it.

**Return to work meeting:** After any absence of three or more working days, your manager will hold a brief return-to-work meeting. This is not disciplinary; it is to ensure you are supported.

---

## 6. Other Leave Types

**Parental leave:** BrightPath offers enhanced maternity and adoption leave: 12 weeks at full pay, followed by 27 weeks at Statutory Maternity Pay (SMP) rate. Paternity/partner leave is 2 weeks at full pay. Shared parental leave is available; contact people@brightpath.io for the SPL policy document.

**Bereavement:** 5 days of paid bereavement leave for an immediate family member (partner, parent, child, sibling); 2 days for other close relatives. Additional unpaid leave may be agreed with your manager.

**Jury service:** Paid in full for up to 10 working days. For longer jury service, BrightPath will top up jury subsistence to your normal salary for up to 6 weeks.

**Medical appointments:** Up to 6 hours per year for medical, dental, and optical appointments during working hours. Book appointments outside core hours where possible.

**Volunteer days:** 2 paid volunteer days per year. Submit a request via hr.brightpath.io at least 5 working days in advance.

## 7. Probation

**Duration:** 3 months for all new employees.

**Review:** A formal probation review takes place at the end of month 2 (informal check-in) and at the end of month 3 (formal sign-off). Your line manager and a People & Culture representative will attend the month-3 review.

**Outcome:** Probation is either passed, extended (once, by up to 6 weeks), or not passed. You will receive written confirmation within 5 working days of the review.

**Entitlements during probation:** You accrue leave and pension contributions from day one. However, the following benefits are not available until probation is passed: compressed weeks (Section 2), full remote requests (Section 3), enhanced sick pay (Section 5 applies only if probation is passed; before then, SSP applies from day 1 of illness), private health insurance (Section 9).

---

## 8. Performance Reviews

**Cycle:** Annual review in November–December, covering 1 October to 30 September. A mid-year check-in takes place in April.

**Process:** (1) Self-assessment form submitted by employee (2 weeks before review); (2) Manager completes assessment; (3) 60-minute review meeting; (4) Goals set for next year; (5) Written summary shared within 10 working days.

**Pay reviews:** Linked to the annual review. Merit increases take effect 1 January following the review. The company budgets for salary increases annually; increases are not guaranteed.

---

## 9. Benefits

**Pension:** BrightPath contributes 5% of qualifying earnings; the minimum employee contribution is 3%. We use Nest. You are auto-enrolled from day one; opt-out window is 30 days from your start date.

**Private health insurance:** Provided by AXA PPP from the date probation is passed. Covers employee and up to two dependants. Enrol via the AXA portal (link in your onboarding pack).

**Cycle to work scheme:** Available year-round via Cyclescheme. Maximum bike value £1,000 (tax-free). Submit requests to finance@brightpath.io.

**Season ticket loan:** Interest-free loan of up to £3,000 for an annual public transport season ticket. Available after 6 months of service. Repaid via monthly salary deduction. Apply via finance@brightpath.io.

**Learning budget:** £1,000 per employee per year for professional development (courses, certifications, conferences). Approved by your line manager. Submit receipts via the expenses system within 30 days of purchase.

**Employee Assistance Programme (EAP):** Free, confidential counselling and support (up to 6 sessions per year) through Health Assured. Freephone: 0800 028 0199.

## 10. Expenses

**Approval:** Expenses over £50 require pre-approval from your line manager. Below £50, approval can be retrospective.

**Submission deadline:** All expense claims must be submitted within **30 days** of the expense being incurred. Claims submitted after 30 days will not be reimbursed without a written exception approved by the Finance Director.

**Travel:** Economy class for all flights under 5 hours. Standard class for rail. Use public transport where practical; mileage for personal vehicle use is reimbursed at HMRC Advisory Rates (currently 45p/mile for the first 10,000 miles per tax year).

**Accommodation:** Up to £150/night in London, £100/night elsewhere in the UK. Exceptions require Finance approval.

**Receipts:** All claims require itemised receipts. Card statements alone are not accepted.

**System:** Submit via Spendesk (expenses.brightpath.io). Contact finance@brightpath.io with questions.

## 11. Code of Conduct

All employees must:

- Treat colleagues, clients, and partners with respect regardless of background, identity, or role.
- Maintain confidentiality of client data and BrightPath's proprietary information.
- Declare any conflict of interest (personal or financial) related to their work in writing to people@brightpath.io before engaging.
- Not accept gifts from clients or suppliers worth more than **£25** without prior approval from their manager.
- Not undertake paid secondary employment without written approval from HR.

Violations of the Code of Conduct are subject to the disciplinary process (Section 12).

---

## 12. Disciplinary Process

BrightPath follows a progressive disciplinary process in line with the ACAS Code of Practice:

1. **Informal conversation:** Manager raises the concern verbally.
2. **Formal written warning:** Issued following a formal meeting. Remains on record for 12 months.
3. **Final written warning:** For serious or repeated issues. Remains on record for 24 months.
4. **Dismissal:** For gross misconduct or continued failure to improve.

You have the right to be accompanied at any formal disciplinary meeting by a colleague or trade union representative.

## 13. IT and Data Security

**Password policy:** All company accounts must use a password manager (BrightPath provides 1Password licences). Passwords must not be reused across services.

**Device encryption:** All company laptops must have full-disk encryption enabled. IT will confirm this during device setup; do not disable it.

**Data handling:** Customer data must never be stored on personal devices or personal cloud accounts (Google Drive personal, Dropbox personal, etc.). Use BrightPath's Google Workspace and approved cloud storage only.

**Security incidents:** Report suspected breaches or lost/stolen devices immediately to security@brightpath.io and your line manager. Do not wait.

**Software installation:** You may install software from the approved list (available on the IT portal) without approval. Any other software requires a request to it@brightpath.io.

---

## 14. Leaving BrightPath

**Notice periods:** 1 month during probation (from either side); after probation, notice is as stated in your contract (typically 1–3 months depending on seniority).

**Garden leave:** BrightPath may place employees on garden leave during their notice period. You remain employed and receive full pay but do not attend work.

**Equipment return:** All company equipment must be returned to the nearest office (or shipped at BrightPath's cost) within **5 working days** of your last day. Contact it@brightpath.io to arrange a return.

**Final payslip:** Your final payslip will be issued on the normal payroll date following your last day. Any owed expenses or outstanding leave will be included.

**References:** HR provides employment confirmation references only (dates and job title). Line managers may provide personal references in a personal capacity with your consent.
