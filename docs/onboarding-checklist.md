## Day 1

### Morning (before 11:00)

- [ ] **Collect your access card.** If you are in London: IT desk, Ground Floor Room G4. Manchester: Reception desk, Level 1. Edinburgh: Facilities office, 2nd floor. If you are starting remotely, your temporary access card will be posted; it arrives within 2 working days.
- [ ] **Sign into your company email** (firstname.lastname@brightpath.io). Your temporary password is in the welcome email sent to your personal address. Change it immediately via account.brightpath.io.
- [ ] **Set up 1Password.** You will receive an invite to your company email. Set up your account before trying to access other systems — most portals require 1Password TOTP.
- [ ] **Join Slack.** Accept the invite from your company email. Join the following channels: `#general`, `#team-[your-team-name]`, `#random`, `#onboarding-2026`. Say hello in `#general`.
- [ ] **Meet your buddy.** They will reach out via Slack before noon. If you haven't heard by 11:00, message people@brightpath.io.
- [ ] **Book your probation calendar.** In Google Calendar, block out "Probation Review — Month 2" and "Probation Review — Month 3" from today's date. Your line manager will send formal invites, but blocking your own calendar now avoids conflicts.

### Afternoon (11:00–17:00)

- [ ] **IT orientation call.** IT will send a 30-minute calendar invite for this afternoon. They will walk you through: device encryption check, software install list, VPN setup, and how to raise IT tickets via it.brightpath.io.
- [ ] **Read the Employee Handbook.** Available at hr.brightpath.io/handbook and in the `#onboarding-2026` Slack channel. You will be asked to confirm you have read it by end of Week 1.
- [ ] **Complete emergency contact form.** On hr.brightpath.io under "My Profile → Emergency Contacts". Takes 5 minutes. Required within 3 working days.
- [ ] **Review your contract.** Check your role, salary, notice period, and start date are correct. Raise any discrepancies with people@brightpath.io within 5 working days.
- [ ] **Line manager 1:1.** Your manager will have a 30-minute welcome call or meeting scheduled. Confirm your role objectives, your team's current priorities, and who you'll be working with most closely.

---

## Week 1 (Days 2–5)

- [ ] **Complete payroll setup.** Log in to hr.brightpath.io and complete your bank details, tax code form (P45 or Starter Checklist), and pension opt-in/opt-out by **end of day 3**. Late submissions may delay your first payslip.
- [ ] **Set up Google Workspace.** Enable Google Drive, Google Calendar (share with your manager), and Google Meet. Your Google account is the same as your email.
- [ ] **Confirm pension enrolment.** You are automatically enrolled in Nest from day one. To opt out, you must do so within 30 days via the Nest portal (link in hr.brightpath.io). If you want to increase contributions above the 3% default, update this via Nest directly.
- [ ] **Read and sign the IT Security Policy.** Available at it.brightpath.io/policies. E-sign and submit by end of Week 1.
- [ ] **Set up VPN.** Required for accessing internal systems outside the office. IT will provide your credentials during the IT orientation call. Install the GlobalProtect client on your laptop.
- [ ] **Team introductions.** Your buddy will schedule a 45-minute team intro meeting in Week 1 where you meet the wider team. Bring a 2-minute "who I am and what I'm here to do" intro.
- [ ] **Book a coffee with your buddy.** 30 minutes, informal. Your buddy is your go-to for "how do we actually do things here" questions — not just the written policies.
- [ ] **Acknowledge the Employee Handbook.** Submit the acknowledgment form on hr.brightpath.io by **end of day 5**. This is mandatory.
- [ ] **Confirm your office days.** Agree with your line manager which two (or more) days per week you will be in the office. Add them as recurring calendar events.

---

## Weeks 2–4

- [ ] **Complete mandatory e-learning.** Three modules on hr.brightpath.io: (1) Data Protection & GDPR — 45 minutes; (2) Information Security Awareness — 30 minutes; (3) Equality, Diversity & Inclusion — 30 minutes. All three must be completed by **end of month 1**. Completion is tracked and reported to your manager.
- [ ] **Set up expenses account.** Register on Spendesk (expenses.brightpath.io) using your company email. Your manager will approve your account within 2 working days of registration. Reminder: submit claims within 30 days of each expense.
- [ ] **Enrol in private health insurance** (if probation is already passed or once it is passed). Use the AXA PPP portal link provided in your onboarding pack. If you need the link resent, email people@brightpath.io.
- [ ] **Review your learning budget.** You have £1,000 per year from day one (not prorated). Discuss with your line manager what you'd like to spend it on this year. Approval is required before booking.
- [ ] **Agree your probation objectives.** Your line manager must confirm 3–5 clear objectives for your probation period by end of Week 2. These form the basis for your Month-3 review.
- [ ] **Shadow at least one colleague.** Arrange to shadow a team member for a half-day to understand how client work is run end to end. Coordinate with your buddy.

## 30-Day Checkpoint

Your line manager will hold a 30-minute check-in at approximately day 30. You don't need to prepare a formal document, but it is helpful to reflect on:

1. Do you understand what success looks like in your role for the next 3 months?
2. Are there tools, processes, or knowledge gaps you need support with?
3. Is there anything about the way we work that surprised you (positively or negatively)?

This is not the formal probation review — that happens at Month 2 and Month 3.

---

## 60-Day Checkpoint (Informal Probation Review)

At approximately day 60, your manager will hold the informal probation check-in. They will:

- Review progress against your probation objectives.
- Share early feedback on strengths and any development areas.
- Confirm whether you are tracking to pass probation at Month 3.

If there are concerns, they will be raised here with enough time to address them before the formal review.

---

## 90-Day Review (Formal Probation Sign-Off)

The formal probation review takes place at approximately day 90. Format:

- 60-minute meeting with your line manager and a People & Culture representative.
- You complete a self-assessment form (sent to you 2 weeks before the review).
- Written outcome shared within 5 working days: Pass, Extend, or Not Pass.
